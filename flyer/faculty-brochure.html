About Mini Pupper

Mini Pupper by MangDang is an affordable, open-source four-legged robot priced under $1,000 — making it one of the most accessible quadruped platforms for education and research. It supports ROS2, computer vision, SLAM, and can be integrated with Google Gemini for AI-powered interactions and natural language control.
